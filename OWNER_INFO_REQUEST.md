# Information to Request from Viet Before Final Release

Use this checklist after the first visual concept is approved.

## Identity
- Preferred display name
- Preferred job title
- Current city/location wording
- Professional headshot
- LinkedIn URL
- Public email (optional)

## About
- 2–4 sentence self-introduction
- What kind of work he wants to be known for
- Key professional strengths

## Experience
For each company:
- company name
- title
- start/end dates
- short responsibilities
- major achievements that can be made public

## Projects
For each public project:
- project name or safe generic title
- location
- Solar PV/BESS capacity
- role
- responsibilities
- outcome/impact
- approved project image
- whether client/company name may be public

## Education & professional development
- university
- degree/major
- dates
- certifications actually obtained
- training courses worth mentioning

## Preferences
- Any website he likes
- Whether he prefers portrait photo or energy-project hero image
- Whether he wants the site positioned for networking, personal branding, job opportunities, business development, or all of the above
