# Project Brief

## Project
Viet Le — Personal Portfolio

## Positioning
A professional personal website for a renewable-energy specialist whose work relates to:
- Solar PV
- Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)
- Project Development
- Project Management
- Vietnam's evolving energy market
- Energy transition / sustainability

## Primary audience
- industry peers
- business partners
- recruiters / hiring managers
- project stakeholders
- renewable-energy professionals
- international contacts from LinkedIn

## Primary outcomes
Visitors should quickly understand:
1. Who Viet is.
2. What energy domains he works in.
3. What kinds of projects and responsibilities he has handled.
4. His professional journey.
5. How to connect with him.

## Site format
One-page portfolio / professional profile.

Recommended section order:
1. Header / Navigation
2. Hero
3. About
4. Expertise
5. Experience
6. Selected Projects
7. Professional Activities / Industry Engagement
8. Education & Development
9. Contact / LinkedIn CTA
10. Footer

## Tone
- professional
- concise
- international
- technically credible
- not boastful
- human and approachable

## Scope exclusions for v1
- blog
- CMS
- authentication
- admin dashboard
- dark theme
- multilingual system
- newsletter
- complex contact form
- 3D scenes
